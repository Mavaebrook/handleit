DuoText BY HANDLEIT

Text in English or Spanish. DuoText translates incoming messages into your preferred language and lets you send English, Spanish, or both in group conversations. Translation runs on your phone after a one-time language download.

In development — download not yet available
// Roadmap

PLANNED FEATURES

On-Device EN/ES Translation

Translation runs entirely on your phone — no cloud service is contacted. After a one-time language model download, English and Spanish translation works offline.

Incoming Message Translation

Incoming messages are translated into your chosen reading language, so conversations in the other language are readable at a glance.

Outgoing Translation Preview

Before you send, DuoText shows a preview of the translated outgoing message so you can confirm what the other person will read.

"Both" Mode for Groups

In group conversations, "Both" mode sends a single shared group MMS carrying a labeled English + Español body, so every member reads in their own language.

SETUP (PLANNED)

The following describes the intended install flow. It is planned and not yet available — there is no download at this time.

  • Download the APK when it becomes available.
  • Allow install from browser when prompted by Android.
  • Select DuoText as your SMS app in Android settings.
  • Grant permissions required for messaging.
  • Download language models for the one-time on-device translation setup.
