// What it handles

The office work that piles up while you're on a job.

CALLS & LEADS

Every missed call gets a text back

Missed callers hear from you right away. New leads are asked for their address, service and timing, and voicemails land on one list.

QUOTES

Quotes that follow themselves up

Drafts from your walkthrough notes and photos, reminders when a customer goes quiet, and line items kept in step with supplier prices.

CREWS & JOBS

A schedule that bends, not breaks

When weather, a long job or a sick tech changes the day, it proposes reschedules, texts customers once you approve, and sends your team tomorrow's routes.

INVOICES & CASH

Get paid without the awkward calls

Friendly reminders on overdue invoices, receipts sorted, and a heads-up before payroll or a big bill.

REVIEWS

More reviews, better replies

Asks happy customers for a review the day after the job and drafts calm replies to the tough ones.

EQUIPMENT

Nothing quietly expires

Tracks vehicle and equipment service, insurance renewals and licenses, and books the shop when something is due.

// You stay in charge

It drafts, plans and reminds. You decide.

Your name is on every message and every invoice, so nothing that matters goes out on its own. You choose what runs automatically and what waits for you.

  • Hold to approveSending, paying and rescheduling wait for a deliberate press on your phone. Letting go cancels.
  • Limits for your teamLet a lead tech or crew lead approve small purchases up to an amount you set. Anything bigger comes to you.
  • A record of everythingEvery action is logged with what was sent, to whom and who approved it.
  • Texts from a proper business numberMessages go out from a registered business texting number, not someone's personal phone.
  • Your team doesn't need an appTechs and crew leads get routes and updates by text and reply the same way.

// FAQ

Questions owners ask

Do I have to switch from the software I use now?

No. Office Manager works alongside the scheduling, quoting and invoicing tools you already pay for. It reads from them and drafts the work; you keep using what you know.

Will it text my customers without asking me?

Only the messages you choose to pre-approve, like an instant reply to a missed call. Everything else waits for your OK, and you can change those settings anytime.

Does it answer the phone for me?

During the pilot it texts back missed calls and organizes voicemails into a lead list. You or your office still take the live calls.

What does my team have to do?

Nothing new. Techs and crew leads get their routes and reschedules by text and can reply to mark jobs done or ask for changes.

Who is it for?

Owner-run trades and home-service businesses with a few people in the field: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, lawn care and landscaping, cleaning, pest control, pool service, roofing and more. If your phone rings while your hands are busy, it's built for you.
