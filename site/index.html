A FEW THINGS, CLEARED UP

Before you ask.

Straight answers. No technical maze.

Is this another app I have to set up?

The proposed service includes setup and ongoing management. Your Customer Hub brings services, messages, documents and account details together.

What stays under my control?

You agree the services and permissions before setup. Website content and social posts come to you for approval before publication.

Do I have to replace all my tools?

We start with what you already use and check what can be connected. Any replacements or extra services are discussed before you commit.

What’s available, and what does it cost?

This is an early-access offering. Specific services, pilot options, usage limits and pricing are confirmed for your business before setup. Please contact us to discuss your setup; online ordering is not available yet.
