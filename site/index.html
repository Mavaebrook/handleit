01

Chosen for relevance.

Your goals, instructions, and connected information give each assistant context for what belongs on its dashboard: a reply to review, a deadline approaching, or a task that needs a decision.

02

Prioritized by what needs you.

Urgency, due dates, pending approvals, and blocked work help determine what rises to the top. Each item makes clear what happened, why it matters, and the next step.

03

Maintained by the assistant responsible.

Communications, business, finance, schedule, development, and media each have their own view of active work. Each assistant keeps its view current as tasks progress, with important items surfaced in Command.

04

You keep the final say.

Open an area for the full picture, talk to its assistant to adjust priorities, and review proposed actions. Surfacing an item on your dashboard does not itself authorize an action.
