This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and disclose your personal information when you visit handleit.online or use our SMS-based conversational AI assistant.

1. Information We Collect

When you opt in to our SMS service through our website form or by texting a keyword to our phone number, we may collect:

  • Phone Number: Your mobile phone number, used solely to send and receive SMS messages related to your inquiry.
  • Message Content: The content of text messages you send to us and our automated responses.
  • Form Data: Any information you provide through our web forms (such as your name and inquiry details), along with the time you gave consent.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Respond to your sales and customer support inquiries by SMS.
  • Power our conversational AI assistant to answer questions about our services in real time.
  • Keep records of consent and communications for quality and compliance purposes.

3. SMS Opt-In Data

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. SMS consent and phone numbers are never shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We share information only with the service providers that deliver our messages (such as our SMS carrier platform), and only as needed to provide the service.

4. SMS Program Details

  • Opt-In Consent: You must explicitly consent to receive SMS messages by checking an unchecked checkbox on our web form. Consent is not a condition of purchase.
  • Message Frequency: Message frequency varies based on your inquiry and conversation.
  • Opt-Out: Reply STOP, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, QUIT, or END at any time. We stop all SMS messages to your number as soon as we receive an opt-out request.
  • Help: Reply HELP for assistance at any time.
  • Rates: Message and data rates may apply.

5. Data Retention

We keep SMS conversation logs and related data only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, or as required by law. You may ask us to delete your data at any time by contacting us.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. SMS data is transmitted over encrypted channels (TLS).
